Core Features

Everything you need to
run your operations.

Smart Booking Experience

Allow clients to easily book services, consultations, appointments, or reservations through a seamless branded experience. Smart availability, buffer rules, intake forms, and instant confirmations — all built into the flow, not bolted on.

Client Management

Manage clients, booking history, notes, statuses, communication, and onboarding from one dashboard. Your entire client relationship in one place.

Operational Dashboard

Track bookings, revenue, pending requests, follow-ups, tasks, and business activity in real time. The view of your business you've always wanted.

Automated Workflows

Reduce manual admin work with automated confirmations, reminders, follow-ups, and notifications. Set it once — the system runs itself.

Team & Internal Management

Assign bookings, manage schedules, monitor operations, and streamline internal coordination. Keep your whole team aligned without the back-and-forth.

Payments & Invoicing

Integrate payment collection, invoices, deposits, and booking confirmations into one workflow. Quotes become invoices become receipts — all on rails.
